# What's New in Version 4.42.1

**Released:** November 3, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Privacy page
Anyone can open the privacy page and read what TheoShift keeps for a volunteer, what the phone app stores on the phone, and who to ask if a record should be changed or removed.

**How to use it:**
1. Open the privacy page from the phone app profile, or go to the privacy address on the website.
2. Read the short statement. No sign-in is required.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The phone app is still not in the App Store or Google Play.
- Signing out removes the sign-in on that phone. The event keeps the volunteer record. Ask an overseer to change or remove it.

---

## 📞 Need Help?

- Check the **Help Center** in the app
- Contact your system administrator
- Send feedback using the **Send Feedback** button
