# What's New in Version 4.38.0

**Released:** October 31, 2026

---

## ✨ My volunteers

Keymen and overseers have **My volunteers** on the phone app. The list shows each person assigned to them, with their assignments, count times, check-in, and lanyard. **My positions** limits the list to the positions they oversee.

When a brother is assigned to a position after documents were published to everyone, he receives those documents.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The app is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Use the website until someone gives you the app.
- Reload the app if My volunteers is not on More yet.
- No action is required if you only use the website.
