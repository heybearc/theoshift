# What's New in Version 4.33.11

**Released:** October 28, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### See how many people or stations are selected
When you check one or more rows, a bar at the bottom of the screen shows the count, such as “3 volunteers selected.” It stays visible while you scroll. The same count appears next to Volunteers List. Positions, the day board, IVS approvals, and count-time station assignment use the same bar.

### Page size stays on the Volunteers list
Showing, Per page, and the page buttons stay under the volunteer list, including when everyone fits on one page and after you choose All. Per page is available on a phone as well as on a computer.
