# What's New in Version 4.42.0

**Released:** October 31, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Enter your count on the phone
On **Assignments**, the count that is open for you is listed above your stations. Tap it, enter the number, and submit. A group count is one total for every station in that group. The page updates on its own when the next count opens.

**How to use it:**
1. Open the TheoShift app and sign in with your email link.
2. On **Assignments**, tap the attendance count.
3. Enter the number and submit.

### Contacts, station diagram, and announcements
**More** includes **Contacts** so you can call or email your overseer or keyman. Assignments can open the station diagram. A pinned announcement stays on Assignments until you choose **I've read this**.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### A submitted count stays locked
After you submit a count, it turns grey. An overseer, keyman, or event staff member can change it. The next count opens one hour after the previous count time.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The phone app is still not in the App Store or Google Play. Use the copy someone gives you, or the website.
- Reload the app to see the new count, contacts, and announcement screens.
- The website volunteer dashboard follows the same count rules.
