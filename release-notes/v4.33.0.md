# What's New in Version 4.33.0

**Released:** October 19, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Bulk edit on the Positions day board
You can select stations on the day you are viewing and update them together. That includes area and status, shift templates, new shifts, how many volunteers a shift needs, and the station overseer and keyman.

### Set shift overseer and keyman for a whole day
Choose the stations, open Bulk edit, and set the shift overseer and keyman for every shift on the day you are looking at. Someone already in that role is replaced.

### The rest of the classic tools are on the day board
Filters for overseer and role, the list of available volunteers, auto-assign for the whole event, export, and clear-all actions are on the day board. Clear-all asks you to confirm first. The classic list and grid layout is still there if you want it.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Positions**.
2. Choose **Select visible**, then **Bulk edit**.
3. Set the shift overseer, keyman, or other station details, then save that section.
