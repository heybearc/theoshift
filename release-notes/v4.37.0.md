# What's New in Version 4.37.0

**Released:** October 30, 2026

---

## ✨ More of your volunteer tools on the phone

The TheoShift app now has a **More** tab:

- Reply to an availability request
- Open documents published to you
- Confirm your email and phone
- Check volunteers in early, when you are on that event’s early-entry roster

Assignments and chat stay where they were. The website is unchanged.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The app is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Use the website until someone gives you the app.
- Reload the app if More is not on the tab bar yet.
- No action is required if you only use the website.
