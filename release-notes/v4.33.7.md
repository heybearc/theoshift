# What's New in Version 4.33.7

**Released:** October 26, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Collapsed stations stay one row
On Positions, a closed station shows its name, how full it is, and the **Station** box. The shifts appear when you open the station.

Check **Station** to bulk edit, delete, or set the overseer and keyman for this day’s shifts. Open the station to select one shift.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Positions**.
2. Leave a station closed to scan the day, or open it to work on its shifts.
3. Check **Station**, then choose **Set overseer/keyman**, **Bulk edit**, or **Delete**.
