# What's New in Version 4.33.14

**Released:** October 29, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Count groups save when a counter is blank
Saving a count group no longer fails with "Invalid payload" when the primary or secondary counter is left empty. The group stays after you leave the page. Each group still needs at least one station.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Open Assign Counters, save the group, then leave and come back to confirm it is still there.
- No other action is required.
