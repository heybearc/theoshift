# What's New in Version 4.33.10

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Assign an overseer or keyman from Bulk Edit
On Volunteers, select people and open Bulk Edit. Assign Overseer and Assign Keyman now list the brothers who are overseers and keymen for this event. Remove is still there when you need to clear one.

## 🔧 Improvements

### Admin lists on a phone
Users, Locations, and Feedback stack down the screen. Names, addresses, and actions stay on the page, and the buttons are easier to tap.
