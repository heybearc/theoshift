# What's New in Version 4.33.9

**Released:** October 26, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Volunteer notes stay saved
On Volunteers, a note you save on a person’s profile shows on their card and in View Details. Open Edit again and the note is still there. Update stays on the screen while you scroll the form.

A blank notes column in a spreadsheet import no longer erases a note that is already saved.

---

## 🔧 Improvements

### Easier on a phone
Volunteer names and email addresses wrap instead of being cut off. The selection box is easier to tap. On Positions, station names wrap, and Assign, More, and the station actions stack down the screen.
