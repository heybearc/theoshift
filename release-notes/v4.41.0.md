# What's New in Version 4.41.0

**Released:** October 31, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Who had this badge?
On Lanyards, enter a badge number to see everyone who has had it on this event, with the phone number and the times it went out and came back. Each row also has **History**. An available badge shows who had it last.

Checking a badge in still clears the current name on the row. The history stays.

**How to use it:**
1. Open the event and go to **Lanyards**.
2. Under **Who had this badge?**, enter the number.
3. Or choose **History** on that badge's row.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Checkouts that were returned before this update were not kept, so those names are not in the list.
- The next checkout is kept, including a badge that is out now when you check it back in.
