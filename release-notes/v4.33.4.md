## 🐛 Shift select checks the shifts

### The box beside the station selects that day's shifts
On Positions, the checkbox next to the station name now selects every shift at that station for the day. Each shift’s **Select** box checks with it, and the bar offers **Set overseer/keyman**.

The checkbox labeled **Station**, on the right, is the one for bulk edit or delete.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Positions**.
2. Check the box beside a station to select its shifts for that day, or check **Select** on one shift.
3. Choose **Set overseer/keyman**.
