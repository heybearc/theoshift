# What's New in Version 4.32.4

**Released:** September 30, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Volunteers list stays on your filters
When you were viewing Inactive (or another quick filter) and typed in search, changing a volunteer used to jump the list back to Active with a blank search.

Edits now stay on the same view, and a short confirmation appears when the save succeeds.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Your search, Inactive/Active/All cards, and other filters stay put after you update a volunteer.
- Adding a new volunteer or importing a CSV still refreshes the page, but those same filters come back with it.
- No action is required.
