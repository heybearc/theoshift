# What's New in Version 4.35.0

**Released:** October 30, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Support role
Sisters who help with an event can be given the Support role. They sign in on the Oversight tab and can manage volunteers, positions, and assignments on the events they are added to. They cannot create or delete events, and they cannot change who else has access.

**How to use it:**
1. On Users, set the person's role to Support and send the invite, or change an existing account.
2. On the event, open Permissions and grant Support.
3. Ask them to sign out and sign in again on the Oversight tab.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Support is for help with one or more events. It is not an overseer title.
- Someone who was temporarily set to Overseer so they could work before this release can be changed to Support. They need to sign out and sign in again.
- No other action is required.
