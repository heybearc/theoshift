# What's New in Version 4.33.2

**Released:** October 19, 2026

---

## 🐛 Oversight lists stay on this event

When you choose an overseer or keyman on Positions, the list is limited to people marked in that role on this event. Other volunteers on the roster stay out of those lists.

This applies when you set station oversight, when you set a shift overseer or keyman, and when you set those roles in bulk edit.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Positions**.
2. Expand a station and choose **Oversight**, or set the shift overseer or keyman.
3. The names you see are the overseers or keymen already marked on **Volunteers** for this event.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action is required.
- Mark someone as an overseer or keyman on the Volunteers page if they should appear in these lists.
