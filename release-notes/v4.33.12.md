# What's New in Version 4.33.12

**Released:** October 28, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### The station keyman shows on the card
When a keyman is assigned to a station, that name appears on the station row next to the overseer. Open the station and each shift shows the station keyman when that shift does not have its own.

### Filled counts the shift
The filled count, such as 0/1 or 1/1, counts volunteers on the shift. An overseer or a keyman does not fill that slot.
