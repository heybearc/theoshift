# What's New in Version 4.33.13

**Released:** October 29, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### One count on the volunteer dashboard
When you are assigned to more than one attendance count, the dashboard shows only the current one. The next count replaces it at that count’s scheduled time. If two counts are less than an hour apart, the next one waits until an hour after the earlier count so both forms are not on screen together.

### The Volunteers page opens
The Volunteers page loads again for events with a large assignment list.

### The shift shows who is assigned
On the day board, an open shift lists the assigned volunteers on one line. The overseer and keyman stay labeled under that line.

### Checked stations are included
Set overseer or keyman uses the stations you checked.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Refresh the volunteer dashboard when the next count time arrives.
- No other action is required.
