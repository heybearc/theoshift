# What's New in Version 4.44.0

**Released:** November 5, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Sign out a lost phone
If a phone with TheoShift is lost, you can end that sign-in from a phone you still have, or from the website.

**How to use it:**
1. On the website, open **Phones** from your dashboard or profile.
2. In the phone app, open **More**, then **Phones signed in**.
3. Sign out the phone you lost, or sign out every other sign-in.
4. That phone stops working the next time it reaches TheoShift.

### Sign-in code
Most volunteers still sign in with the email link. If you were given a sign-in code, enter it with your email and choose **Sign in with code**. Leave the code blank if you do not have one.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Your email sign-in link still works.
- Signing out on a phone you have still signs out that phone.
- Ask an overseer if your email is not on a volunteer record.
