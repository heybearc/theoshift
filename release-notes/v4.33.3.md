## 🐛 Shift select and shift-click

### Select a shift without opening the station
On Positions, each shift for the day has a **Select** box. You can check it even when the station’s assignment details are collapsed. **Select shifts** still checks every shift on that day.

### The last box in a shift-click stays checked
On Volunteers, hold Shift and click another checkbox to select the rows in between. The box you click now shows its check, along with the rest of the range. The same fix is on IVS Approvals.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Positions**. Check **Select** on the shifts you want, then **Set overseer/keyman**.
2. On **Volunteers**, click one checkbox, hold Shift, and click another. Every box in that range stays checked.
