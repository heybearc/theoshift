# What's New in Version 4.33.5

**Released:** October 24, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Export the volunteers you are looking at
On Volunteers, **Export list** downloads a spreadsheet of the people who match the filters you have set. That includes search, status, congregation, overseer, keyman, availability, and forms of service. People on later pages are included.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Volunteers**.
2. Set the filters you want.
3. Choose **Export list**.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Shift-click checks the last box
Hold Shift and click another checkbox to select the rows in between. The box you click stays checked, along with the rest of the range. This works on Volunteers, IVS Approvals, Positions (including each shift’s **Select** box and the box beside the station), Documents, and Count Times.

**How to use it:**
1. Check one box.
2. Hold Shift and check another box in the same list.
3. Every box from the first through the last stays checked.
