# What's New in Version 4.33.1

**Released:** October 19, 2026

---

## 🐛 Choose shifts and skip placeholder emails

### Choose which shifts get an overseer or keyman
On the Positions day board, select the shifts you want, then choose **Set overseer/keyman**. You can also pick shifts inside Bulk edit. Shifts you leave unchecked stay as they are.

### Availability email skips placeholder addresses
When you request availability, addresses that contain “changeme” are left out of the send. The confirm message tells you how many were skipped. If every selected address is a placeholder, nothing is sent.

**How to use it:**
1. Open an event and go to **Positions**.
2. Choose **Select shifts**, check the shifts you want, then **Set overseer/keyman**.
3. On **Volunteers**, placeholder addresses are skipped automatically when you request availability.
